The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first. A booking for 7 April goes out as 4 July. This is the fix #4471 already queued.

hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. At the tenants on booking-v3, people type what the hint shows, month first, and the form reads it day first. Only reports through the form, only at those tenants. The parser fix does not touch it: the hint has to change.

one-day: the date moves by one day, it does not swap. “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” The desk already read these five as a one-day move (desk-notes.json), but the table on #4471 still counts them under parser. Day and month are not swapped in any of them, so the parser fix will not change them. It looks like a time-zone conversion, and it needs its own owner.

Why they were merged
